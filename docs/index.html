House rules
  • Each row is a plate that holds one word, up to 5 letters, left to right with no gaps.
  • Put the letter at either end of a plate, or drop it on a letter already there to swap it.
  • When a plate spells a word of 3 or more letters, it's served and you score.
  • Playing with two, you take turns one letter at a time, whether or not it makes a word.
  • Keep turning a plate into new words, each one another course: TIN → TINE → TILE → TILED. Every course adds a bonus.
  • A plate with only one or two letters on it isn't a dish yet, so it isn't checked.
  • The Q always comes with its U. The QU plate takes one spot but spells two letters, so QU·I·T is QUIT.
  • Serve something that isn't a word and it gets sent back: you lose 25 and the plate is cleared.
  • Don't want a letter? Skip it. It's gone for good, and that counts as your turn.
  • Each word can be served only once per game. If a spot would make a word that's already been served, the letter can't go there.
  • A regular game ends when all 40 letters are used.
  • Turn on All you can eat when you start a new table and the letters keep coming. When you are done, choose Close the check in the menu.
  • The Daily Special deals everyone the same letters each day, with three specials to spell for a big bonus. Every special can be made from that day's letters. You get one try, with no undo, and a fresh table at midnight.

Scoring: add up the letter values, then ×10 for a 3-letter word, ×15 for 4, ×25 for 5, and ×35 for a 6-letter Qu word. Each extra course adds 15. In the Daily Special, specials add 100 (4 letters) or 200 (5 letters).
