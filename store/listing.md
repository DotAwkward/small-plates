## Main store listing

**App name** (30 characters max)
Small Plates: Word Game

**Short description** (80 characters max)
Build words one letter at a time, then turn each one into the next course.

**Full description** (4,000 characters max)

Small Plates is a cozy word game for one or two players, made for the few spare minutes you didn't plan for: waiting on dinner, a long line, the couch.

The kitchen sends out one letter at a time. Put it on one of four plates, swap it for a letter that's already there, or skip it. When a plate spells a word, it's served and you score. Then keep going: turn TIN into TINE, TILE and TILED, and every new course on the same plate earns a bonus.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Table for one: chase your best score.
• Table for two: pass the phone across the table and take turns, one letter each.
• The Daily Special: everyone gets the same letters each day. One try, no undo, and a result card to share with friends.
• All you can eat: the letters keep coming and the specials keep changing until you close the check.

LITTLE THINGS THAT MAKE IT FUN
• Today's specials: spell the chef's words for big bonus points.
• The Qu plate: Q always comes with its U, so QUIET and SQUAT are fair game.
• Sent back: serve something that isn't a word and you lose points and the plate is cleared.
• Each word can only be served once per game, so think ahead.
• Stats for your best dish, longest chain, streaks and more.
• A short, friendly How to play the first time you open it.

MADE TO BE NICE TO YOU
• Plays completely offline.
• No accounts. Your games and stats stay on your phone.
• One small banner ad at the bottom that never covers the game. Nothing pops up between rounds, nothing makes you wait, and you never have to watch an ad to unlock anything.
• Light and dark looks, plus sound and vibration you can switch off.

Small Plates is a Dot Awkward game: small games for odd moments.
